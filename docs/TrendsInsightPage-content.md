## What We Suggest

### Section Title
What we suggest

### Subtitle
Here are some recommended actions based on your insights

### Recommendations

#### 1. Create a week 1 re-engagement campaign
Retention drops significantly after the first week. Build a "here's what you missed" digest or in-app notification that triggers after 3-5 days of inactivity to encourage return visits.

#### 2. A/B test card insights prominence
36% click-through rate shows room for improvement. Test different ways to highlight top insights more prominently—consider visual treatments, placement, or personalized recommendations.

#### 3. Implement proactive feedback prompts
Add a feedback prompt that appears after users view 3-5 insights, rather than relying on passive discovery. Test different trigger points to find the optimal moment for feedback collection.
